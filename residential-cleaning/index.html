Residential Cleaning

A beautifully maintained home—without carrying the entire workload yourself.

From recurring maintenance to detailed deep cleaning, our residential services are designed to make your home feel cared for, comfortable, and easier to manage.

Request a Residential Estimate

Recurring Cleaning

Weekly, biweekly, and customized recurring schedules. Recurring service helps maintain a consistent standard throughout the home. After the initial visit, the cleaning plan can be adjusted to reflect the household's priorities.

Deep Cleaning

Deep cleaning is intended for properties requiring more time and detail than a standard maintenance visit. It may be recommended before recurring service begins.

One-Time Cleaning

One-time service may be available for special occasions, seasonal cleaning, returning from travel, preparing for guests, or addressing accumulated cleaning needs.

What a residential visit may include

Kitchen Illustrative

  • Clean accessible countertops
  • Wipe accessible exterior cabinet surfaces
  • Clean sink and faucet
  • Clean exterior of major appliances
  • Clean stovetop according to safe surface requirements
  • Clean microwave interior when included
  • Remove ordinary household trash
  • Vacuum and mop accessible floors

Bathrooms Illustrative

  • Clean and sanitize sinks and faucets
  • Clean mirrors
  • Clean toilets
  • Clean accessible shower and tub surfaces
  • Wipe counters and exterior cabinet surfaces
  • Vacuum and mop floors
  • Remove ordinary household trash

Bedrooms & living spaces Illustrative

  • Dust accessible surfaces
  • Wipe selected high-touch surfaces
  • Clean mirrors where included
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Mop appropriate hard floors
  • Empty ordinary wastebaskets
  • Make beds using linens left by the client when included

Throughout the home Illustrative

  • Dust accessible ledges and furniture
  • Spot clean selected reachable surfaces
  • Clean accessible baseboards when included
  • Remove cobwebs within safe reach
  • Vacuum stairs
  • Clean interior glass doors where included

Exact inclusions depend on the selected service, condition of the property, accessibility, allotted time, and written estimate.

What clients should prepare

  • Secure valuables, medications, firearms, and confidential materials
  • Provide safe entry instructions
  • Disclose pets
  • Identify delicate surfaces
  • Remove excessive clutter where possible
  • Disclose damaged fixtures
  • Identify areas that should not be entered
  • Ensure utilities are available
  • Disclose insects, mold, bodily fluids, hazardous materials, or unsafe conditions before arrival
Services we do not currently provide: biohazard remediation, crime-scene cleaning, mold remediation, asbestos removal, pest-control services, sharps disposal, medical-waste disposal, cleaning of bodily fluids or hazardous substances, and hoarding remediation unless separately assessed and approved in writing.
