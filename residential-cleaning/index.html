What a standard cleaning may include.

The exact scope depends on the confirmed service.

Kitchen Typical

  • Clean accessible countertops
  • Clean sink and faucet
  • Wipe accessible exterior cabinet surfaces
  • Clean exterior appliance surfaces
  • Clean stovetop where appropriate
  • Clean microwave interior when included
  • Remove ordinary household trash
  • Vacuum and mop accessible floors

Bathrooms Typical

  • Clean sinks and faucets
  • Clean mirrors
  • Clean toilets
  • Clean accessible shower and bathtub surfaces
  • Wipe counters
  • Clean exterior cabinet surfaces
  • Remove ordinary household trash
  • Vacuum and mop floors

Bedrooms and living areas Typical

  • Dust accessible surfaces
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Mop appropriate hard floors
  • Empty ordinary wastebaskets
  • Make beds using customer-provided linens when included
  • Clean mirrors when included

Throughout the home Typical

  • Dust accessible furniture and ledges
  • Remove reachable cobwebs
  • Clean accessible high-touch surfaces
  • Vacuum stairs
  • Clean interior glass doors when included

Exact inclusions depend on the selected service, property condition, accessibility, and written estimate.

Product preferences
