Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you book.

What does eco-conscious cleaning mean?

It means we make deliberate product choices based on cleaning performance, surface compatibility, fragrance, household use, and environmental considerations.

Do you use organic products?

We do not describe every product as organic. Product claims vary by manufacturer and formulation.

Are all products plant-based?

Plant-based products may be used where appropriate, but product selection depends on the cleaning task, surface, condition, and customer requirements.

Are your products non-toxic?

We avoid broad non-toxic claims. Cleaning products should always be used according to their instructions and kept away from children and pets.

Can I request fragrance-free products?

Fragrance-conscious options may be available depending on the requested service, product availability, surface, and cleaning requirement.

Do you use bleach?

Routine cleaning does not automatically require bleach. Product choices depend on the cleaning need, customer request, and sanitation requirement.

Are the products safe for pets and children?

Products are selected thoughtfully, but no cleaning product should be treated as harmless. Customers should disclose pets, children, sensitivities, and concerns before service.

Do you bring supplies?

Supply arrangements are confirmed before service. Yasaymo Eco Cleaning may provide selected products and equipment based on the requested service.

How does residential pricing work?

Residential customers can build an estimate using the online pricing calculator. Final pricing is confirmed after review.

Is the booking immediately confirmed?

No. The appointment is confirmed only after the final invoice is accepted and the required deposit is paid.

When is payment due?

First-time and one-time residential customers pay a 50% deposit within 24 hours after receiving the invoice. The remaining balance is due no later than the service date and before cleaning begins.

Do recurring customers receive discounts?

Recurring savings begin with the second completed service and depend on maintaining the agreed schedule.

Do you clean commercial offices?

Yes. Commercial pricing is customized after reviewing the property and requested service.

Do you clean medical offices?

Select medical, dental, veterinary, and professional offices may be accepted after a facility assessment. Specialized clinical and hazardous services are excluded.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Tucson and select surrounding communities, including Oro Valley, Catalina Foothills, Marana, East Tucson, Rita Ranch, and Vail. Availability depends on location, scheduling, and requested service.

Do you clean homes with pets?

Pet-friendly homes may be accepted. Customers must disclose all animals, behavioral concerns, access restrictions, and pet-hair conditions before service. For everyone's safety, customers may be asked to secure animals during service.

Can you guarantee stain removal?

No. Some staining, discoloration, wear, mineral buildup, damage, mold, or odors may not be removable through routine cleaning.

What if I am unhappy with the service?

Please contact us promptly with specific details. We will review the concern against the agreed service scope and determine an appropriate resolution.

Still have a question?

We are happy to talk it through before you book anything.
