Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you request service.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Tucson and select surrounding communities. Availability depends on the property location, requested service, and current scheduling capacity.

Do you provide free estimates?

Estimate requests can be submitted without charge. Some homes or facilities may require photographs, a phone consultation, or an on-site assessment before final pricing is provided.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on factors such as property size, condition, number of rooms, service type, frequency, access, pets, requested add-ons, and estimated labor time.

Do you charge by the hour?

Services may be priced by the project, visit, labor hour, or customized service agreement. The applicable structure will be explained before confirmation.

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Supply arrangements will be confirmed with each client. Some properties may require client-approved or surface-specific products.

Can I request fragrance-free products?

Clients should disclose fragrance sensitivities and product preferences during the estimate process. Requests are reviewed based on product availability and the needs of the property.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. Access arrangements can be discussed before service. Clients remain responsible for providing accurate and safe entry instructions.

Will I have the same cleaner?

We aim to provide consistency whenever scheduling permits, but specific personnel cannot be guaranteed unless included in a written service arrangement.

How should I prepare?

Secure valuables and confidential items, disclose pets and delicate surfaces, remove excessive clutter where possible, and provide clear access and parking instructions.

Do you clean homes with pets?

Pet-friendly homes may be accepted. Clients must disclose all animals, behavioral concerns, access restrictions, and pet-hair conditions before service. For the safety of the pet and cleaning personnel, clients may be asked to secure animals during service.

What is included in a standard cleaning?

The scope depends on the selected service and written estimate. A typical visit may include kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, floor care, and ordinary waste removal, but exact inclusions must be confirmed.

Do you move furniture?

Cleaners may move only lightweight items when it is safe to do so. Heavy furniture, appliances, and delicate objects are not moved unless explicitly approved.

Do you clean high areas?

Only areas safely reachable with approved equipment are included. Cleaners should never be expected to use unsafe ladders or climb on furniture.

Do you provide biohazard cleaning?

No. We do not provide biohazard remediation, sharps disposal, medical-waste handling, crime-scene cleaning, or hazardous-material cleanup.

Can you guarantee stain removal?

No. Some staining, discoloration, wear, mineral buildup, damage, mold, or odors may not be removable through routine cleaning.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation and rescheduling terms are provided before the first confirmed appointment.

What happens if I am unhappy with the service?

Please contact us promptly with specific details. We will review the concern against the agreed service scope and determine an appropriate resolution.

Still have a question?

We would be pleased to help you decide whether our service fits your property.
