Residential Pricing and Booking

Build your estimate, then choose a date.

Build your estimate, choose an available date, and submit your booking request. Pricing shown is an estimate — final pricing is confirmed after review.

Most residential visits range from $139 to $659, depending on the home's size, condition, service type, room count, and selected add-ons. Build your estimate below for personalized pricing.

Homes over 4,000 sq ft and properties requiring assessment. These are quoted individually after a walkthrough so the scope and time are right for the property. Contact us to arrange an assessment.

How pricing and payment work.

Pricing Residential

  • Base price is set by home size and service type
  • Each size includes a set number of bedrooms and bathrooms
  • Additional rooms are charged at a published rate
  • Recurring savings begin with the second completed service
  • The first visit is priced as an initial or one-time cleaning
  • A minimum residential service charge of $129 applies
  • Homes not cleaned recently begin with a deep cleaning

Payment Deposit

  • Submitting a request does not confirm an appointment
  • We review the request and send a final invoice
  • First-time and one-time bookings require a 50% deposit
  • The deposit is due within 24 hours of the invoice
  • The balance is due before cleaning begins on the service date
  • Your booking is confirmed once the deposit is received

Questions before booking?

We are happy to talk through scope, product preferences, and scheduling before you commit to anything.
