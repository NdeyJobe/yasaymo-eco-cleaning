How a commercial engagement begins.

1

Facility assessment

We review the layout, requested areas, traffic patterns, operating hours, access needs, and any stated requirements.

2

Written scope

The proposal identifies approved areas, tasks, frequency, exclusions, products, access instructions, and communication procedures.

3

Site-specific checklist

Approved tasks are organized into a repeatable checklist tailored to the property.

4

Ongoing communication

A designated contact can report concerns, request adjustments, and coordinate access or scheduling changes.
